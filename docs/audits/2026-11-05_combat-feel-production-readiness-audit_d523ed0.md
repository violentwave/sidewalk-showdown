## B. Findings

Severity: **Critical / High / Medium / Low / Info.** Labels: `CODE-CONFIRMED` (verified by reading), `RUNTIME-REPRODUCED` (verified by probe against the real sim), `DESIGN HYPOTHESIS`, `REQUIRES HUMAN PLAYTEST`.

### B1. Knockdown is a fully vulnerable, zero-agency state → true infinite (Critical, RUNTIME-REPRODUCED)

- **Evidence:** `fighter_base.gd:415-437` — `is_invulnerable_to()` only grants invulnerability in `WAKE_UP` (plus authored reversal invuln in `ATTACK`, which no current move uses). `State.KNOCKDOWN` (30 frames) has **no invulnerability at all**. `fighter_base.gd:466` — `is_blocking()` returns false for `KNOCKDOWN` and `WAKE_UP`. The `KNOCKDOWN`/`WAKE_UP` state branches (`fighter_base.gd:144-149, 229-234`) never call `_try_start_attack()`, so the defender cannot act. `match_root.gd:357-383` — `_poll_hit()` has no defender-state guard for strikes.
- **Reproduction (probe T1):** land Peggy `crouch_heavy` (knockdown), then re-time `crouch_heavy` into the 30-frame knockdown window. Result: **10 consecutive re-knockdowns, 1000 → 445 HP, defender state never leaves KNOCKDOWN.** Variant T1b: defender holds back (block direction) the entire time — **zero blocks occur; the loop still runs.**
- **Player-facing consequence:** one knockdown converts to a guaranteed kill with no counterplay (not blockable, not techable, no action available, no invulnerability). This is a hard infinite, violating GDD §5.10 ("No infinite combos allowed," `GDD.md:515`).
- **Regression test proposal:** "a strike cannot connect with a defender in `KNOCKDOWN`" (or: knockdown grants invulnerability until `WAKE_UP` frame 0, which preserves the designed meaty game exactly); plus a loop-guard test that times a knockdown move into the knockdown window twice and asserts the second hit whiffs.

### B2. Throws connect on knockdown — loopable but techable (High, RUNTIME-REPRODUCED)

- **Evidence:** `fighter_base.gd:482-491` — the `THROW` path in `receive_hit()` guards only airborne defenders and tech; there is no guard for `KNOCKDOWN`/`HITSTUN`/`BLOCKSTUN`. Wake-up throw invulnerability (`wakeup_throw_invuln_frames = 8`) is counted from `WAKE_UP` frame 0, never during the preceding 30-frame knockdown.
- **Reproduction (probe T2/T2b):** grab → re-time grab into the knockdown window: **5 consecutive throw loops, 1000 → 558 HP.** With the defender buffering grab, the loop throw **is teched** (both fighters enter `TECH`, no damage) — so this loop has counterplay, unlike B1.
- **Note:** with the current frame data, throws cannot land during hitstun or blockstun (every move is frame-negative or neutral on hit/block for the attacker: e.g. light +4 on hit but total 12 vs grab startup 5 misses the stun window). The knockdown case is the reachable one.
- **Player-facing consequence:** knockdown → throw loops unless the defender knows to buffer grab; combined with B1 this makes knockdown completely degenerate.
- **Regression test proposal:** "a throw cannot grab a defender in `KNOCKDOWN`" (or an explicit owner decision that it can, documented in PROPOSALS).

### B3. Buffered inputs age out during hitstop and blockstun (High, RUNTIME-REPRODUCED)

- **Evidence:** `fighter_base.gd:115-121` — `_poll_input()` runs *before* the hitstop early-return, so the buffer ages while the state machine is frozen. Aging at `:646-649` (+1/frame, erased at 20), consume windows at `:651-655` (`window + 1`, i.e. 6 for normals, 8 for specials). `_stun_logic()` (`:221-227`) likewise never consumes buffered attacks during hitstun/blockstun.
- **Reproduction (probe T3):** heavy hit lands (hitstop 6 on both). A light "pressed" on the first frozen frame is age 6 when the sim resumes and never fires (the attacker is still in heavy recovery, and by the time heavy ends the buffer is erased). Probe T8: a light pressed on the first blockstun frame — blockstun measured **18 frames** (authored 12 + 6 hitstop freeze) — buffer age 18 at stun end, **dropped**.
- **Player-facing consequence:** inputs pressed during freezes and blockstun are silently eaten. There is no blockstun buffering, so punishes and blockstrings require frame-perfect presses *after* stun ends — this reads as unresponsive controls and is one of the most common "the game feels stiff" causes in young fighting games.
- **Regression test proposal:** press light on blockstun frame 1, release stun, assert the light starts within 2 frames; same for a press during hitstop.

### B4. Corner clamping leaves unresolved pushbox overlap (Medium, RUNTIME-REPRODUCED)

- **Evidence:** `match_root.gd:385-400` — `_separate_pushboxes()` splits the overlap 50/50, then clamps both fighters to the stage; when one fighter is at the bound, its clamp re-creates the overlap that was just removed.
- **Reproduction (probe T4):** p1 pinned at `left_bound`, boxes overlapping 10px → after separation, **5.00px residual overlap** (mid-stage control: 0.00px).
- **Player-facing consequence:** fighters can stand slightly "inside" each other in the corner; throw and close-normal ranges are effectively inflated there; potential side-switch/facing edge cases.
- **Regression test proposal:** place p1 at the bound with a known overlap, step one frame, assert pushbox intersection ≤ 0.

### B5. Starting an air attack zeroes horizontal momentum (Medium, RUNTIME-REPRODUCED)

- **Evidence:** `fighter_base.gd:376` — `_start_move()` sets `velocity.x = 0.0` unconditionally (grounded or not). During `ATTACK`, `_air_movement()` (`:193-202`) no longer runs, and `_attack_logic()` decelerates `velocity.x` (`:208`). Air drift therefore only exists while no attack is in progress.
- **Reproduction (probe T5, corrected with forward held):** holding forward mid-jump, vx = 95.0 with no attack; starting the air heavy zeroes vx to 0.0 instantly and it stays 0.
- **Player-facing consequence:** jump-ins stop dead horizontally the moment a button is pressed. The air probe's reach envelope (connections only at d ≤ 40; d = 60/80 mostly whiff) is partly a *consequence of this line*, not only of hitbox data. Whether to keep (floaty, readable anti-air) or restore drift is a design decision.
- **Regression test proposal:** assert `velocity.x` is preserved (or intentionally zeroed) on air-move start — whichever the owner decides, pin it in a test.

### B6. Landing discards all remaining aerial recovery (Medium, RUNTIME-REPRODUCED; already documented as spec)

- **Evidence:** `fighter_base.gd:246-262` — on touchdown, `current_move = null` and `ATTACK → LAND`, which lasts 2 frames (`:134-137`). `finish_move_whiff()` (whiff meter) only runs when a move ends in the air (`:213-215`), so landing mid-move also skips the whiff-meter grant.
- **Reproduction (probe T6):** air heavy started 8 frames before landing (17 authored recovery frames remaining) → actionable **2 frames** after touchdown.
- **Player-facing consequence:** air attacks are far lower-commitment than their frame data claims; whiffed jump-ins cost almost nothing. PROPOSALS §3 already specifies the fix (`MoveData.landing_recovery` + `_landing_check` branch); it is spec-only.
- **Regression test proposal:** land mid-recovery, assert actionable frame count equals authored `landing_recovery` (once added).

### B7. Wake-up frames ~6–11: vulnerable with no block and no action (Medium, RUNTIME-REPRODUCED; partly intended)

- **Evidence:** `fighter_base.gd:146-149` (WAKE_UP branch has no actions), `:466` (cannot block in WAKE_UP), `fighter_data.gd` defaults (`wakeup_invuln_frames = 6`, `wakeup_throw_invuln_frames = 8`, `wakeup_frames = 12`).
- **Reproduction (probe T7):** a light meaty timed to wake-up frame ~6 lands clean on a defender holding back (HP path 1000 → 925 knockdown → 890 meaty).
- **Reading:** the 6-frame invuln + meaty-able tail is *exactly* what PROPOSALS §1 designed, and the tests cover it. What is not covered by the proposal is that the vulnerable tail also **cannot block** — mainstream 2D fighters allow holding block on wake-up. This is a design decision the owner must make, not a clear bug.
- **Label:** RUNTIME-REPRODUCED (behavior) + **REQUIRES HUMAN PLAYTEST** (whether wake-up block should exist).

### B8. Cancel windows and combo limits are implicit, not authored (Medium, CODE-CONFIRMED)

- **Evidence:** `fighter_base.gd:297-320` — `_try_cancel()` hard-codes: light → light/heavy/special, heavy → special, hit-confirm only, cancelable at any frame after startup until the move ends. `_damage_scale()` (`:591-601`) hard-codes 100/85/70/55% scaling; `_tick_combo()` (`:668-672`) resets combos after 40 frames. `move_data.gd` has no cancel fields; GDD §5.3 (`GDD.md:390`) requires a per-attack "Cancel Rules" field, and GDD §5.6 (`:436`) specifies counter-hit +2 hitstop — neither exists. There is also **no juggle limit**: airborne hits re-apply knockback and re-stun freely (bounded only by the 55% damage floor and gravity).
- **Consequence:** tuning combo routes or adding a third fighter requires editing engine code, contradicting the project's own "fighters are data" architecture rule (README). GDD-vs-code mismatch on the scaling floor: GDD §5.10 says minimum 35% (`GDD.md:526`), code floors at 55%.
- **Regression test proposal:** once authored, assert a move's cancel list matches data; assert scaling floor matches the documented value.

### B9. Hurtboxes are aliases of pushboxes (Low, CODE-CONFIRMED; documented)

- **Evidence:** `fighter_base.gd:690-691`. GDD §5.4 specifies separate hurt/hit/push/throw/proximity boxes; the README's "separate combat boxes" overstates this. PROPOSALS states it correctly ("one box, no separate hurtbox").
- **Consequence:** no extended-limb hurtboxes, no per-move hurtbox shaping (e.g. lean-back poses); crouch hurtbox = 65%-height pushbox. Fine for greybox; will matter when sprites with poses arrive.
- **Note:** B9 is a *capability* gap, not a wrong behavior — nothing currently computes a wrong result from it.

### B10. Hit resolution order effects beyond ordinary trades (Low, CODE-CONFIRMED)

- **Evidence:** `match_root.gd:342-355` — both hits are polled before either is applied (trades and double-KOs are correct and tested). Residual order effect: p1's hit applies first, so if it KOs p2, `p2`'s already-polled hit still damages p1 *after* `p1` has been forced into `WIN` (`match_root.gd:138`); a lethal reply correctly converts to the double-KO draw, but a non-lethal one leaves the round winner standing in `HITSTUN` during the KO phase (cosmetic).
- **Also verified:** `_separate_pushboxes()` runs before `_resolve_hits()` each frame, so separation can push a fighter out of (or into) a hitbox the same frame — deterministic, but worth knowing when authoring meaty ranges.
- **Regression test proposal:** trade-into-WIN-state test asserting the winner's post-trade state is `WIN` (or documenting the hitstun-during-win as intended).

### B11. Informational (no action required)

- Hitstop also freezes the wake-up invuln countdown (`state_frame` does not advance) — documented in PROPOSALS §1 and covered by a test.
- An invulnerable brush awards whiff meter at move end — documented in PROPOSALS §1 as a deliberate call.
- `find_move()` stance fallback (air/crouch → stand) is dormant (both fighters have full row sets) but could silently produce a standing move if a future fighter lacks a row — worth a data-validation test when roster expansion starts.

## Air Probe Audit (`game/tests/air_probe.gd`)

**Does it measure real match behavior?** Partially. It instantiates the real `MatchRoot`, real `FighterBase` state machines, real hitbox/hurtbox geometry, and real gravity/jump arcs — but it sets `phase = MATCH_END` (air_probe.gd `_boot`), which disables `_resolve_hits()` and `_separate_pushboxes()`. Consequences:

- Section 3 (trade matrix) is **geometry + state timing only**: no `receive_hit`, no hitstop, no knockback, no tech. A "trade" in the probe does not execute the real same-frame trade path (which *is* covered by combat tests for grounded moves).
- Section 2 (damage per arc) is a **data read**, not a measured outcome: single-hit damage vs the fighter's own jump heavy (0.5 / 1.0 / 1.125). PROPOSALS §3 metric 2 actually specifies *jump-in damage plus guaranteed follow-ups divided by the defender's anti-air damage* with a target ratio ≤ 0.8 — **that ratio is not computed**. No follow-ups are measured and defender anti-air reward is absent from the output.
- Section 4 (blocked advantage) is computed arithmetic (defender blockstun minus attacker recovery). The arithmetic is legitimate because hitstop freezes both fighters equally — but note the real measured blockstun in the live sim is *authored blockstun + hitstop* (probe T8: 18 = 12 + 6); the arithmetic cancels this correctly only because both sides freeze.
- Section 5 (whiff-punish window, min 3 frames) correctly exposes the landing-cancel problem (B6).

**Both roles and both sides?** No. Only Peggy attacks and only from the left side (`p1_x` fixed, facing 1); Richard is only ever the defender; no mirrored run; anti-airs are tested from stand only.

**Invalid overlap/spacing setups possible?** Yes — fighters are placed by hand and pushbox separation is disabled in `MATCH_END`, so overlapping or interpenetrating setups are expressible and would not be caught. `_reset_pair` restores health/state each iteration, which is sound.

**Informational vs assertions:** PROPOSALS §3 defines four acceptance targets. Against the current committed data:

| §3 target | Current probe output | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Air special reach ≤ 60% of jump heavy's connecting frames | air_special 3/8 vs jump_heavy 3/8 (equal) | Not met |
| Damage ratio (air-in : anti-air) ≤ 0.8 | **not measured** (spec gap) | Unknown |
| Blocked jump-in advantage ≤ −2 | air_special **+4** | Not met |
| Whiffed air recovery ≥ 8 frames | min **3** | Not met |

**Recommendation:** keep the reach envelope informational until the B5 (momentum) and B6 (landing recovery) decisions land, since both change the envelope. Promote to assertions afterwards: blocked advantage and whiff window should become hard thresholds per §3, and the missing air-in : anti-air ratio metric should be added (it is the spec's core balance question). Also add defender-role and mirrored-side coverage, and either run the matrix through `Phase.FIGHT` or rename the section to "geometry-only trade estimate."

## D. Revised Two-Fighter, One-Stage Polish Roadmap

Ordered so that correctness fixes land before feel changes, and feel changes before presentation (presentation on top of broken states wastes art).

**Stage 1 — Correctness (B1, B2, B4):** knockdown strike/throw guards + loop regression tests; corner separation remainder. Exit: the probe loops are impossible; corner overlap is 0.

**Stage 2 — Input feel (B3):** stop buffer aging during hitstop and stun states (or equivalently extend consume windows by the frozen duration). Exit: buffered inputs survive freezes; blockstun buffering works.

**Stage 3 — Air game decisions (B5, B6, B7):** land `MoveData.landing_recovery` per PROPOSALS §3; decide air momentum; decide wake-up block. Re-run the air probe; promote §3 targets to assertions. Exit: whiff window ≥ 8f, blocked advantage ≤ −2, envelope per target.

**Stage 4 — Combat depth (B8):** authored cancel windows (per-move data), juggle/OTG rules, counter-hit (+2 hitstop per GDD §5.6), damage-floor reconciliation. Exit: tuning combos requires no engine edits.

**Stage 5 — Presentation:** SFX first (hits, blocks, KO, UI), then hit sparks and screen shake on heavy/special hits, KO slow-motion, METHtality choreography (camera zoom + banner timing), then sprite animation wiring for the two fighters, then HUD pixel font (GDD Phase 4 direction).

**Stage 6 — Vertical-slice shell:** character-select mockup, controller support (GDD must-have), training-mode stub reusing the debug overlay, final balance pass with the probe assertions gating CI.

## E. Recommendations by Category

**Correctness**
- Ship the three PRs in section F; add regression tests for B1/B2/B3/B4 before touching any balance number.
- Add a data-validation test that every fighter has a full move-row set (guards the dormant stance fallback, B11).

**Combat design** (all DESIGN HYPOTHESIS until playtested)
- Knockdown game: with B1 fixed, okizeme becomes meaty/throw/bait as PROPOSALS §1 intended — playtest before adding more.
- Counter-hit (+2 hitstop, GDD §5.6) is the cheapest "intensity" lever that rewards aggression into aggression.
- Consider dash/backdash (GDD §5.2) — currently walk-speed-only neutral limits pressure and throw shimmying.
- Intensity levers that read as "more fun" cheaply: hitstop variance per move strength (already data), KO slow-motion, screen shake on knockdowns. Do not raise damage to make the game feel faster — fix B3 (eaten inputs) first; stiffness is usually buffer loss, not damage.

**Presentation**
- Audio is the single biggest missing "feel" system (documented); wire hit/block/whiff SFX before art.
- METHtality: the rules are real; the 30-frame freeze + banner is honest scaffolding — camera zoom and a 2-frame hit-spark burst would carry most of the missing spectacle.

**Accessibility**
- Controller support (GDD must-have, currently absent).
- Input remap UI (GDD §4.6 requires it; only the generator exists).
- Colorblind-distinct silhouette colors/outline options; hitstop/flash-reduction toggle; the shared-keyboard P1/P2 split is already thoughtful — keep it when gamepads arrive.

**Performance**
- Nothing measured is a concern (silhouette rendering, 60 Hz fixed sim, two fighters). The air probe's ~2–4 min runtime is fine for CI since it's a separate non-gating job. The `ProjectSettings.save()` physics-tick regeneration trap documented in KNOWN_ISSUES remains worth a CI check.

## F. Three Proposed PRs (dependency order)

**PR 1 — Knockdown hardening (fixes B1, B2).**
Smallest effective change: in `is_invulnerable_to()` (or as an explicit guard in `_poll_hit()`), treat `State.KNOCKDOWN` as strike-and-throw invulnerable until `WAKE_UP` frame 0 — the meaty game as designed (PROPOSALS §1) is untouched, since meaties target `WAKE_UP` frames ≥ 6, not the knockdown itself. Add tests: strike-on-knockdown whiffs; throw-on-knockdown whiffs (or techs, per owner decision); the probe loops from T1/T2 now fail to loop. Update PROPOSALS §1 with the decided rule. *(METHtality's "opponent in KNOCKDOWN" gate is unaffected — it checks state before spending, and the finisher is itself a strike; if the owner wants METHtalities to hit knocked-down opponents, exempt `meth` rows explicitly in the guard.)*

**PR 2 — Freeze input-buffer aging during hitstop and stun (fixes B3).**
In `_poll_input()`, skip the aging loop while `hitstop_frames > 0` (and optionally while in `HITSTUN`/`BLOCKSTUN` — the same "frozen state machine" argument applies). Alternative if the owner prefers visible aging: extend `_consume()` windows by the accumulated freeze duration. Add tests: a light pressed on blockstun frame 1 fires at stun end; a press during hitstop fires on resume. No balance data changes.

**PR 3 — Corner separation remainder (fixes B4).**
In `_separate_pushboxes()`, after clamping the pinned fighter, apply the un-clamped leftover overlap to the free fighter (push the pusher). Add test: corner setup resolves to ≤ 0 overlap; mid-stage behavior unchanged (existing tests stay green).

*Follow-up (not one of the three):* `MoveData.landing_recovery` per PROPOSALS §3 §"What it would break", which unblocks the air-balance targets and the air-probe assertion promotion.
