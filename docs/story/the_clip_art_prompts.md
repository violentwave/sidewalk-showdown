# The Clip — Panel Art Prompts (v2, pixel art)

## How to use
- Attach as reference images in every generation: TrippVlogz,
  PegLegPeg, WhiskeyRichard (character sprites) and BusStopNight.png
  (environment palette).
- Paste GLOBAL STYLE + CHARACTER SHEET + RECURRING PROPS into every
  prompt.
- Export panel_01.png ... panel_15.png at 16:9. Native 320x180, or
  640x360 for crisper detail (the game downsamples with
  nearest-neighbor).

## GLOBAL STYLE
Chunky retro pixel art matching the supplied character sprites: large
visible square pixels, hard pixel edges, NO anti-aliasing, NO smooth
gradients, NO blur, one consistent pixel size across the whole image
(never mix pixel sizes). Thick dark outlines on characters. Limited
palette: dirty teal night sky, sodium-yellow streetlight, sun-faded
asphalt grays, neon orange and acid green accents. Grimy Florida
sidewalk mood. 16:9 single scene. ABSOLUTELY NO text, letters, numbers,
speech bubbles, captions, logos with lettering, or watermarks anywhere
(the game overlays all lettering). Keep the top-left third of the frame
visually simple and uncluttered, because the caption box sits there.

## CHARACTER SHEET (match the supplied sprites exactly)
TRIPP VLOGS: friendly-faced guy in his early 30s, messy brown hair, full
brown beard, wide open-mouth grin. Blue t-shirt with white block
lettering on the chest (render the lettering as unreadable white pixel
marks), blue jeans, white sneakers with bright cyan soles. Usually holds
a black smartphone with a yellow "!" notification. Never heroic, never
cool.
PEG LEG PEGGY: green knit beanie with a white stripe, long curly auburn
hair, wide determined grin showing teeth, brown coat open over a red
shirt, olive pants, ONE slim tan peg leg on one side. Practical, wiry.
WHISKEY RICHARD: red bandana-style cap, long dark hair, dark beard with
a gray patch, stern scowl, navy jacket open over a tan undershirt,
ripped brown pants, a green glass bottle in one hand. Sturdy, scrappy,
weathered.

## RECURRING PROPS (keep identical across panels)
- PROBABLY LEGAL ENERGY can: tall slim can, acid-green body, one orange
  lightning bolt, NO lettering, subtly wrong-looking. Appears in panels
  1, 8, 9, 11, 12, 15.
- THE BENCH: dented green slatted bus-stop bench. Appears in panels 3,
  4, 5, 13.
- TRIPP'S RIG: phone duct-taped to a mop, plus a round ring light.

## PANELS
1. Cluttered studio apartment at night, lit only by ring light and
   monitor glow. TRIPP slumped in a gaming chair among crushed Probably
   Legal Energy cans and takeout boxes. A wall display shows a tiny
   downward-trending graph (abstract, no digits). Wide shot, Tripp small
   and deflated.
2. Extreme close-up of a phone screen: an upload grid of three video
   thumbnails (a man eating at a gas pump; a man sitting on a curb in
   thrift-store clothes; a man filming a doorway at night). Each has a
   tiny red view graph sinking toward zero. No legible text.
3. Night bus stop. TRIPP livestreaming with arms wide, mid-plea, phone
   duct-taped to a mop leaning on the bench, ring light blazing. Far
   behind him, small and slightly dimmer, PEGGY and RICHARD start
   squaring up. Tripp dominates the foreground.
4. Same bus stop, closer. RICHARD planted on the bench, arms spread
   claiming territory, green bottle in hand. PEGGY stands over him, arms
   crossed, unimpressed. Exaggerated argument poses, pixel anger marks.
5. Wide action panel: full brawl behind an oblivious TRIPP who faces the
   camera in serene mid-monologue, ring light behind him like a halo.
   RICHARD mid-swing, PEGGY dodging low, dust cloud and motion lines.
6. Extreme close-up: TRIPP's thumb pressing a zoom icon on a phone, his
   manic eyes reflected huge in the glass. Starburst lines around the
   thumb. The most dramatic framing on page two.
7. Tilted phone screen: a rocketing upward view graph, reaction
   thumbnails multiplying like gremlins. Abstract viral explosion,
   speed lines. No legible text.
8. Close-up of a garish email popup on a phone: the PROBABLY LEGAL
   ENERGY can emblem (green can, orange bolt, no lettering) glowing over
   a blurred trash-strewn sidewalk. Legally distinct from any real brand.
9. Daytime parking lot. TRIPP standing ON a folding table, fist raised,
   cheap microphone in hand, a hand-painted bedsheet banner behind him
   with scrawled paint shapes only (no readable text). A small crowd of
   odd locals holds flyers.
10. Full-frame retro street-poster flyer: a big empty headline band at
    the top (game overlays the text); below it a crooked, one-window-
    boarded house looking suspiciously enthusiastic beside a fan of
    cash. Bottom edge: a dense unreadable squiggle where fine print
    would be.
11. Signup strip at the folding table: an angry man with clenched fists
    and a grudge; a woman squinting at an upside-down flyer; a hopeful
    soul holding a napkin like a hot dog. TRIPP in the background,
    shouting, veins showing, a Probably Legal Energy can in hand.
12. TRIPP straight to camera, hand pressed to his heart, ring light
    behind him like a saint's halo. One hand holds the Probably Legal
    Energy can; the other holds a phone whose screen shows his own
    reflection (he is reading his sincerity off it). A cracked vanity
    mirror at the edge.
13. Tournament bracket wall, Mortal Kombat style: fifteen portrait
    slots. Thirteen are pure black silhouettes with varied outlines (a
    tail, a whistle-wearing cap, a coiled snake, a scooter, big hair, a
    chef-hat shape, a hunched bruiser, and so on). The two center slots
    show PEGGY and RICHARD in full color mid-argument. Below the grid, a
    small emblem of two figures fighting over a bench. Hand-drawn marker
    dollar signs next to the center two slots.
14. Close-up of the bracket's bottom edge: one extra slot with a large
    ominous "?" mark shaped from raw pixels (not a font), torn edges, a
    faint ring-light glow bleeding from behind it. The most sinister
    panel. Let shadow do the work.
15. Splash: loud tournament-poster backdrop with a cheering silhouetted
    crowd, spotlights, confetti, and an abstract subscriber-counter
    display climbing (no digits). A large empty central area for the
    logo overlay. Unhinged and triumphant, slightly falling apart.

## CONSISTENCY CHECKLIST (before accepting an image)
- Same pixel size throughout; no smooth gradients.
- Characters match the sprite references (hair, hats, clothing).
- Top-left third is calm and uncluttered.
- No letters, digits, or logos with text anywhere.
